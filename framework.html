What the framework does

GuideCensus has three parts: a frozen codebook, the production pipeline, and a gold-standard benchmark.

The pipeline

Production runs in four stages, with an optional fifth for versioned corpora.

Five design principles

  1. Freeze before touch.
  2. Two channels, one adjudicator.
  3. The verbatim-quote gate.
  4. Error-rate-driven escalation.
  5. Hash-chained artefacts.

The gold standard: score your extractor

The benchmark ships the frozen ENT Layer-3 dataset and a runnable scorer.
